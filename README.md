# Black/Matrix — English translation for Sega Saturn

**Beta preview · Volunteer playtesters wanted**

This AI-assisted English fan translation beta covers the opening wake-up scene
on the Japanese Sega Saturn rerelease. Expect translation mistakes, awkward
wording, and display bugs. Menus, name entry, later scenes, and videos remain
in Japanese.

## Download and apply the patch

Get the patch from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/retrogamingreloaded/black-matrix-translation/releases).
The release includes instructions and checksums for the Japanese rerelease.
Apply the patch to your own disc dump and open the resulting CUE in your Saturn
emulator.

## Playtest and report bugs

Help us find and correct translation mistakes, broken line breaks, clipped or
overlapping text, and unexpected characters. Please
[report a bug](https://github.com/retrogamingreloaded/black-matrix-translation/issues/new?template=playtest-report.yml)
and **attach a screenshot of the affected screen**. Include the patch version,
emulator or hardware, scene or location, and steps to reproduce. Keep ROMs, BIOS
files, and saves out of issue attachments.
